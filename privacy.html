Privacy Policy

Snipe The Move · last updated October 5, 2026

Who uses the tool

"STM Video" is used only by Snipe The Move, and it connects only to Snipe The Move's own accounts. It has no other users.

What we access

Only what the account owner (us) authorizes on our own accounts: basic account information to confirm which account is connected, the ability to upload our own videos (as drafts or posts), and, where available, the statistics and comments of our own posts.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of other people. We do not track viewers. We do not use any data for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are kept privately on our own computer and used only to upload and manage our own content. They can be revoked at any time from each platform's app settings.

A direct message to @snipethemove.
